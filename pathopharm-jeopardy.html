Pathopharm Review Jeopardy

Built entirely from this site's Pathopharm Review content — all 11 sections, one category each: Pharmacology Foundations; Cellular Adaptation, Infection & Immunity; Hypertension & Hyperlipidemia; Diabetes & Metabolic Syndrome; Coagulation & DVT; Blood Cells & CBC; Fluid & Electrolytes; Pain & Seizures; Dementia, Depression & SUD; GI, GU, Skin & Hypersensitivity; and Stress & Steroids. Every question is NCLEX-NGN/clinical-judgment style with an answer and rationale — nothing from outside this material.

A 3-player game: every game puts all 11 categories on the board in a new order, with 5 questions each (55 total), and difficulty rises from 100 to 500 points within every category. The bank holds extra questions at every value, so each new game draws different ones from the last. Click a value, read the question, then hit Reveal Answer — the answer and rationale won't show until then. Add or subtract points for Player 1, 2, or 3 as your group agrees on who got it. "New Game" shuffles the categories and draws new questions; "Reset Game" replays the current board from scratch.
