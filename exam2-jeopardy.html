A 3-player game: each round randomly draws 3 Week 3 categories + 3 Week 4 categories (30 questions total), with difficulty rising from 100 to 500 points within every category. Click a value, read the question, then hit Reveal Answer — the answer and rationale won't show until then. Award or dock points to Player 1, 2, or 3 as your group agrees on who got it. "New Game" draws new categories and new questions every time; "Reset Game" replays the current board from scratch.
